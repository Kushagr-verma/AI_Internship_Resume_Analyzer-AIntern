How it works

One resume in, four stages of clarity out

Every upload moves through the same pipeline — in order — so what you see is always built on the stage before it.

01
Resume Analysis

We parse experience, projects and coursework into structured skills — no keyword-stuffing games, no guessing.

02
Internship Matching

Your profile is scored against live listings from partner companies, ranked by real fit — not just title keywords.

03
Skill Gap Analysis

For every match, we show precisely which skills you already have and which ones are missing before you apply.

04
Learning Roadmap

A short, ordered list of courses and projects mapped to your gaps — so effort goes where it moves the needle.
